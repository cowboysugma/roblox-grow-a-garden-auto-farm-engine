- Multi-plot simultaneous planting with synchronized animation handling.

- Rust-free watering scheduler that respects in-game cooldowns.
- Detects dry soil states through visual and state-based heuristics.

- Pest and weed mitigation routines triggered automatically.

- Maturity detection tuned for both common and legendary growth timers.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Most automation tools shout. Verdant Ledger whispers. The interface is intentionally calm — soft greens, generous whitespace, and typography that doesn't scream for attention. We believe that a farming companion should feel like a well-kept garden journal, not a cockpit of blinking alarms.

We also believe in **transparency over mystery**. Every automated action is logged, timestamped, and reversible in the settings panel. You should never wonder what the tool just did on your behalf.
